Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

How much does the EZGO RXV 2 cost?
The 2027 EZGO RXV 2 starts at $9,909 for the Freedom model. Pricing varies by trim, powertrain, color, accessories and dealer-added options. Taxes, freight and setup fees are also excluded from the starting MSRP, so the final price will depend on how your RXV 2 is configured.
Is the EZGO RXV 2 available in gas and electric?
Yes. The EZGO RXV 2 is available with three powertrain choices: 48V AC electric, ELiTE lithium and a 13.5-hp gas engine. Valor S and Valor S+ models are available with 48V electric or gas power, while the Freedom RXV 2 is offered with ELiTE lithium or gas.
How fast does the EZGO RXV 2 go?
The EZGO RXV 2 can reach up to 19 mph, depending on the model and powertrain. Freedom configurations can reach approximately 19 mph, while Valor S and Valor S+ gas models are rated at about 12 mph. Actual speed can vary with terrain, passenger load and vehicle configuration.
How many passengers does the EZGO RXV 2 seat?
The EZGO RXV 2 seats two passengers. It uses a two-seat layout designed for golf, neighborhood driving, short trips and everyday personal transportation while keeping the compact dimensions of the RXV platform.
What is the difference between 48V, ELiTE lithium and gas RXV 2 models?
The 48V RXV 2 uses four 12-volt lead-acid batteries, while the ELiTE lithium model uses a single lithium battery that requires no battery watering. The gas RXV 2 uses a 13.5-hp fuel-injected engine with a 5.82-gallon fuel tank. Choose 48V for traditional electric operation, ELiTE lithium for lower battery maintenance, or gas if you prefer quick refueling.
